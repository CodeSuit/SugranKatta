# 🥬 सुगरण कट्टा (Sugran Katta) - Official Website

> **"घरच्या चवीसाठी, वेळेची बचत!"**  
> *Fresh • Clean • Chopped Vegetables | Nahre Ambegaon, Pune*  
> **WhatsApp / Phone:** `+91 9156872582`

A modern, fast, responsive web application for **Sugran Katta (सुगरण कट्टा)**, built to digitize and scale their chopped and uncut fresh vegetable delivery business in South Pune (Narhe, Ambegaon, Dhayari, Katraj, Sinhagad Road, etc.).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Exact Flyer Fidelity & Brand Authenticity**:
   - Features every single product, slogan, USP badge, and contact detail from the original poster.
   - Wooden-board branding emblem with leaf accents, matching the flyer logo.
   - Original Flyer Modal with zoom and download features.

2. **Bilingual Support (मराठी & English)**:
   - Instant language toggle between Marathi (मराठी) and English with Devanagari typography (`Noto Sans Devanagari`).

3. **Complete Categorized Vegetable Catalog**:
   - **कट केलेल्या भाज्या (Cut & Chopped - 200g Pockets)**: Diced Onions, Tomatoes, Green Chilies, Carrots, Capsicum, Shredded Cabbage, Misal Mix Veg, Washed Spinach, Desi Coriander, French Beans, Peeled Potato cubes, Beetroot, Fresh Sprouts, and Salad Platters.
   - **न कट केलेल्या भाज्या (Whole Uncut)**: Fresh carrots, cucumbers, cluster beans (gawar), okra (bhendi), potatoes, brinjals, bottle gourds (dudhi), sweet pumpkin, and more.
   - **पालेभाज्या (Cleaned Leafy Greens)**: Methi, Palak, Shepu, Rajgira, Lal Math, Alu leaves, Curry leaves.
   - **विशेष कॉम्बो (Meal Kits & Combos)**: Morning Breakfast Combo (Poha/Upma), Puneri Misal Kit, and Daily Veggie Kits.

4. **1-Click Automated WhatsApp Checkout**:
   - Customers choose vegetables, packet counts, chopping preference (*बारीक / मध्यम*), and delivery slots.
   - Generates a formatted Marathi/English WhatsApp invoice and sends it directly to `+91 9156872582`.
   - Celebratory confetti on order dispatch.

5. **"वेळेची बचत" Time & Garbage Savings Calculator**:
   - Interactive sliders for family size and cooking frequency showing exact hours saved per week and month.

6. **Pune Delivery Area Checker**:
   - Instant pin code and locality validation for Narhe, Ambegaon (Bk & Kh), Dhayari, Vadgaon, Katraj, Sinhagad Road, Warje, Manik Baug, etc.

7. **Zero-Dependency & Instant Deployment**:
   - Runs out-of-the-box with standard Node.js (`npm start` or `node server.js`).
   - Ready for one-click deployment to Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages.
